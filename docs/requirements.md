# Business Requirements

## Functional Requirements
- FR-1: Deliver the scope defined in the project charter.
- FR-2: Provide regular status reporting to stakeholders.
- FR-3: Ensure data accuracy and auditability.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- NFR-1: Meet agreed service levels.
- NFR-2: Comply with applicable security and privacy policies.
- NFR-3: Be maintainable and well-documented.
